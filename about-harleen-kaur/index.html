Her story

10+ years of international teaching, now in Ottawa

Harleen brings more than 10 years of international teaching experience across Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand and India. She holds a B.Sc. and an M.Sc. and has a background in pharmacy, which gives her a deep, practical understanding of Chemistry and Biology.

She founded QueensCoaching to give high school students the personal attention they rarely get in a busy classroom. Her approach is simple: find the gaps from earlier grades, close them while covering the curriculum, and build the confidence students need for university.

Harleen personally coaches Grade 9–12 Chemistry, Biology and Math, in person in Ottawa and live online.
