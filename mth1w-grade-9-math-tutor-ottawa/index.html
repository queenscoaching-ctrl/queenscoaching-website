MTH1W questions

Does EQAO count toward my child's mark?

Yes. EQAO Grade 9 Math results are included in the final MTH1W course mark. We prepare students for its online format.

What does de-streamed mean?

Since 2021, all Ontario Grade 9 students take the same math course, MTH1W, instead of choosing academic or applied.

What comes after MTH1W?

Most students take MPM2D (academic) in Grade 10, which leads to university math. Others take MFM2P (applied). We help you choose.

When should we start tutoring?

Ideally at the start of Grade 9, or in the summer after Grade 8 to strengthen the basics before high school.
