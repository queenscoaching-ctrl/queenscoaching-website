Ontario final exams

January & June exams, unit by unit

Final exams cover a whole semester. Cramming rarely works, because weak early units make later ones harder. We start with a free assessment to find the weak units, then build a plan that reviews them in order.

Exam dates are set by each school, so we plan backwards from your child's own exam schedule.

  • Free assessment to find weak units
  • Written exam plan within 24 hours
  • Past units reviewed step by step
  • Practice tests in the exam format
  • Personalized notes & stationery included
