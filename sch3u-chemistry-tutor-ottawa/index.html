SCH3U questions

Is SCH3U hard?

It is a big step up from Grade 10 Science because of the math involved. Students who get help with the mole and stoichiometry early usually do well.

Do I need SCH3U?

SCH3U is the prerequisite for SCH4U, which many Ontario health science, life science and engineering programs require.

When should we start tutoring?

At the start of the course, or before the Quantities in Chemical Reactions unit, where most students first struggle.

Can you help with online or summer school SCH3U?

Yes. We follow the same Ontario curriculum whether your child takes SCH3U in day school, summer school or online.
