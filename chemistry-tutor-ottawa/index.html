Why Chemistry is our signature program

Chemistry is where many students hit a wall, and it opens the door to health, science and engineering programs. Founder and lead coach Harleen Kaur holds a B.Sc. and M.Sc. with a background in pharmacy, and personally coaches Grade 11 and 12 Chemistry.

  • A starting check of Grade 10 Science and Math gaps
  • Personalized notes for every unit, kept in a binder
  • All stationery included
  • Practice tests before every school test and the final exam
  • Stoichiometry and equation-balancing drills
  • A monthly progress note for parents

Questions families ask

Which Chemistry courses do you tutor?

Grade 11 SCH3U, Grade 12 SCH4U and Grade 12 College SCH4C, plus Grade 9 and 10 Science. We also coach AP, IB and GCSE Chemistry online.

Is tutoring 1-on-1?

Yes. Every session is one coach and one student, in person in Ottawa or live online.

How much does Chemistry tutoring cost?

Every student's needs are different, so we share pricing after the free assessment, along with a written plan.

Can you help with Waterloo chemistry contests?

Yes. We offer optional practice for the Waterloo Avogadro Exam (Grade 11) and Chem 13 News Exam (Grade 12).
