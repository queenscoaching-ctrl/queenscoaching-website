SBI3U questions

Is SBI3U hard?

It is content-heavy rather than math-heavy, with many new terms. Students who review regularly and practise applying ideas usually do well.

Do I need SBI3U?

SBI3U is the prerequisite for SBI4U, which many Ontario health science, life science and nursing programs require.

What is the difference between SBI3U and SBI3C?

SBI3U is the university preparation course and leads to SBI4U. SBI3C is the college preparation course. We tutor both.

When should we start tutoring?

At the start of the course, or before the Genetic Processes unit, where many students first need extra help.
