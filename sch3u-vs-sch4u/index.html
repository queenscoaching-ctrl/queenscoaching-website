SCH3U vs SCH4U: what changes in Grade 12 Chemistry

A side-by-side guide to Ontario Grade 11 and Grade 12 Chemistry, and the SCH3U skills that decide how SCH4U goes.

Book Free AssessmentWhatsApp us

QueensCoaching parent guide · Updated November 2026

The short answerSCH3U (Grade 11) builds the foundations: bonding, reactions, the mole, solutions and gases. SCH4U (Grade 12) applies them to university-level topics: organic chemistry, structure and properties, energy and rates, equilibrium and electrochemistry. SCH3U is the prerequisite for SCH4U, and most SCH4U struggles trace back to SCH3U skills.

Which SCH3U skills matter most in SCH4U?

  • Stoichiometry and the mole come back in energy changes, rates and equilibrium.
  • Balancing equations becomes balancing redox reactions in electrochemistry.
  • Bonding and molecular shape are the starting point for organic chemistry and structure and properties.
  • Acids and bases from the solutions unit grow into pH and equilibrium calculations.

If any of these felt shaky in Grade 11, it is worth fixing before SCH4U starts. See Is SCH3U hard? for the most common gaps.

Is SCH4U harder than SCH3U?

Most students find SCH4U harder, for two reasons: the topics are more abstract, and each calculation has more steps. But SCH4U is also more predictable. Students who start with strong SCH3U skills often find Grade 12 goes better than Grade 11 did, because the methods are familiar.

Should you take SCH4U if SCH3U was a struggle?

If your teen's university program needs SCH4U, a hard SCH3U year is a reason to prepare, not a reason to drop it. The usual fix is to close the specific SCH3U gaps first (often the mole, stoichiometry or balancing equations) during the summer or the first weeks of the semester.

What parents can do

  • Check which university programs your teen is considering and whether they list SCH4U.
  • Look back at the SCH3U units with the lowest marks. Those are the gaps to close.
  • Start support at the beginning of SCH4U, not after the first test.

Questions families ask

Can you take SCH4U without SCH3U?

SCH3U is the official prerequisite for SCH4U. Schools decide on any exceptions, so ask your child's guidance counsellor.

Do I need SCH4U for university?

Many Ontario health science, life science and engineering programs require it. Always check each program's admission requirements.

When should my teen start preparing for SCH4U?

The summer before, or the first weeks of the semester. Reviewing stoichiometry and balancing equations first makes the biggest difference.
