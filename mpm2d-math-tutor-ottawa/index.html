MPM2D questions

What is the difference between MPM2D and MFM2P?

MPM2D is the academic (Advanced) Grade 10 Math course and leads to MCR3U. MFM2P is the applied (Basic) course with more hands-on problems. We tutor both.

Is MPM2D hard?

It is a big step up in algebra, especially factoring and quadratics. With strong Grade 9 foundations, most students do well.

Why does MPM2D matter?

It is the usual prerequisite for MCR3U, Grade 11 Functions, which leads to the Grade 12 university math courses.

When should we start tutoring?

At the start of the semester, or as soon as factoring or quadratics feel shaky. Early help is much easier than catching up.
