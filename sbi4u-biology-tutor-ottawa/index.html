SBI4U questions

Is SBI4U hard?

It is content-heavy rather than math-heavy. Students who organize the material well and practise applying it usually do very well.

Do I need SBI4U for university?

Many Ontario health science, life science, kinesiology and nursing programs require it. Check each program's admission requirements, and we can help you plan.

Does SBI4U need Chemistry?

SBI4U builds on SBI3U, and the Biochemistry and Metabolism units use Grade 11 Chemistry ideas. We fill any Chemistry gaps as we go.

Can you help with online or summer school SBI4U?

Yes. We follow the same Ontario curriculum whether your child takes SBI4U in day school, summer school or online.
