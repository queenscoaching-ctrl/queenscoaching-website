MHF4U questions

Is MHF4U hard?

It is a step up from MCR3U, with new ideas like logarithms and radians. Students with strong algebra and a steady practice routine usually do well.

Do I need MHF4U for university?

Many Ontario engineering, math, science, computer science and business programs require it, and many also require MCV4U. Check each program's admission requirements, and we can help you plan.

Can MHF4U and MCV4U be taken at the same time?

Yes. The Ontario curriculum says MHF4U must be taken before or at the same time as MCV4U, though many students find it easier to finish Advanced Functions first. We tutor both.

Can you help with online or summer school MHF4U?

Yes. We follow the same Ontario curriculum whether your child takes MHF4U in day school, summer school or online.
