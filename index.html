What makes us different

We fix the basics while we cover the curriculum.

Most students struggle in Grade 11 Chemistry or Grade 12 Calculus because of a gap from years earlier, such as fractions, algebra or unit conversions. We find those gaps first and close them while teaching the current course.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A free session with a foundations check, shared with parents.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written plan with a winning strategy within 24 hours: the gaps to close and the course to cover.

  3. 03

    Coach

    1-on-1 sessions with personalized notes, stationery and practice.

  4. 04

    Report

    A before-and-after progress note for parents every month.
