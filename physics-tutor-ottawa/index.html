How we coach Physics

Physics is where math meets the real world. Students who struggle usually understand the idea but get stuck on the algebra, trigonometry or vectors needed to solve the problem. We coach both at once:

  • A starting check of the Grade 10 Math and Science skills physics depends on
  • Step-by-step problem solving: draw it, list what you know, pick the formula, solve, check the units
  • Free-body diagrams and vector practice until they feel natural
  • Personalized notes for every unit, kept in a binder, with all stationery included
  • Practice tests before every school test and the final exam, plus lab report help
  • True 1-on-1 attention every session, and a monthly progress note for parents

Not sure where the gaps are? A free assessment finds them, and you get a written plan within 24 hours.

Why Physics feels hard

Physics asks students to read a situation, decide which idea applies, and then do the math. If rearranging formulas, trig ratios or exponent laws are shaky, even a well-understood idea turns into a wrong answer. Our exponent laws guide and Is MHF4U hard? cover math skills that show up in SPH4U. Taking Advanced Functions alongside SPH4U helps, and we fill math gaps as we go.
