Questions families ask

Answers before you book

Which courses do you teach?

Every Ontario Grade 9–12 Math and Science course, including MTH1W, MPM2D, SNC2D, SCH3U, SCH4U, SBI4U, MHF4U, MCV4U, SPH3U and SPH4U.

Are notes and stationery really included?

Yes. Every student gets personalized notes for each unit plus notebooks, binders and practice worksheets at no extra cost.

How much does tutoring cost?

Every student's needs are different, so we share pricing after the free assessment, together with a written plan for your child. There is no obligation.

What happens in the free assessment?

We check the foundations your child's course depends on, find the gaps holding their marks back, and send you a written plan with a winning strategy within 24 hours.

Does the EQAO Grade 9 Math assessment affect my child's mark?

Yes. EQAO results are included in the final MTH1W course mark. We prepare students for its online format.

My child follows GCSE, IB or CBSE. Can you help?

Yes. We coach GCSE, IGCSE, A-level, IB, CBSE and AP Math and Science online, in your time zone.

How do online sessions work?

Live 1-on-1 video with a shared whiteboard. Notes are sent digitally, and stationery can be shipped within Canada.
