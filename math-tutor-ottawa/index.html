How we coach Math

High school math is a ladder: each course stands on the one before. A student struggling in Grade 11 Functions is often missing a Grade 9 or 10 skill, like integers, exponent laws or factoring. We fix the basics while we cover the curriculum:

  • A free assessment with a foundations check, shared with parents
  • A written plan within 24 hours: the gaps to close and the course to cover
  • Personalized notes for every unit, kept in a binder, with all stationery included
  • Practice tests before every unit test and the final exam
  • True 1-on-1 attention every session, and a monthly progress note for parents

Not sure where the gaps are? A free assessment finds them, and you get a written plan within 24 hours.
